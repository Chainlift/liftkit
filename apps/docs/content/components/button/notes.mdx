## Variants

Use `fill` for the primary action on a screen, `outline` for secondary actions, and `text` for low-emphasis actions like "Cancel."

## Narrow and wide buttons

A button shrinks to fit the space it's given. If its label is too long, the label is cut off with an ellipsis, and any icons keep their size. When a button has no icons, its label is centered, which matters once the button is wider than its content. Buttons with an icon keep their content aligned to the start.
